02 / About

I design brands, layouts and interfaces that hold up under scrutiny — the kind of work that still looks considered a year after it ships.

My practice sits across four disciplines rather than one: brand identity, print and editorial design, social content systems, and digital product UI. Most of my best work happens at the seams between them — a brand system that has to survive being reduced to an app icon, or a print campaign that gets reformatted for a phone screen an hour after it's approved.

I care about craft at every scale: the kerning on a wordmark, the grid underneath a magazine spread, the easing curve on a button. Clients come to me when they need range across formats without the work feeling like it was designed by four different people.
